From 12 photos in this folder (side views from both banks, a pier close-up, aerials, night lighting).
Best side elevations: `images (1).jpeg`, `images (3).jpeg`; pier close-up: `images (2).jpeg`;

Geometry from OpenStreetMap: see docs/map.md (main span over the main channel, a road across Otdykha island,
a short bridge over the narrow channel to the right bank).

## Shape

- A **concrete deck-arch bridge**: the flat road deck sits on top, the arches are underneath.
- Over the main channel **5–6 arches**, the **largest next to the left (city) bank**, spans getting smaller
  towards the island.
- Each span has **two parallel arch ribs**; between rib and deck stand thin **spandrel columns**, forming a row of
  rectangular openings that gets shorter towards the crown.
- **Massive piers**: reddish-brown rusticated granite at the water line with a pointed ice-breaker upstream,
  grey concrete above.
- Deck: road with sidewalks, a low railing, **tall lamp posts** at regular intervals on both sides.
- Colour: warm grey-beige concrete, darker piers.
- Night: arches lit red, pink and blue (possible later with day/night).

## Game plan

- Straight across the river along +Z (the real bridge runs a little diagonally; kept straight for clean
  collisions), from the embankment next to the museum over the island's east end to the right bank.
- Deck ~5 units above the bank, reached by gentle stepped ramps at both ends.
- Main channel: 4 arch spans decreasing from the left bank; viaduct on columns across the island;
  2 arch spans over the narrow channel.
- Built along local +X so the viewer's front view shows the side elevation; placed with rotationY = −π/2.
